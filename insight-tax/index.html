FAQ

Questions we hear every week

When is my Australian tax return due?

The Australian financial year runs from 1 July to 30 June. If you lodge yourself, your return is due by 31 October. If you are on a registered tax agent's client list before 31 October, you may be eligible for a later due date under the agent lodgement program.

I live overseas. Can you still prepare my Australian return?

Yes. We can work with you online, so it makes no difference whether you are in Brisbane or overseas. Common situations include Australian rental income, residency questions and final returns.

I am a US citizen living in Australia. Do I need you as well as my US preparer?

Usually, yes. As an Australian tax resident you generally need to lodge an Australian return covering your worldwide income, while your US preparer handles your US filing. We coordinate with them so foreign tax credits and offsets are claimed correctly on both sides.

How do I know if I am an Australian tax resident?

Residency depends on several tests, including where you live, how long you are here and your intentions. It is not always the same as your visa or citizenship status. We assess your situation and document the position so you can rely on it.

What should I bring to my appointment?

Please complete our online appointment form beforehand so your details are correctly recorded in our secure database. Bring receipts for deductions, rental and investment statements, and any foreign income records. We pre-fill most income details from the ATO.

Is your fee tax deductible?

In most cases, yes. The cost of managing your tax affairs, including a tax agent's fee, is generally deductible in the year you pay it.

I am behind on my tax returns. Can you help?

Yes. No matter what state your records are in, we tidy up past years, lodge overdue returns and prepare amendments where needed, then keep you up to date from there.

Do you see clients on weekends or after hours?

Yes. We offer weekend and after-hours appointments, in Brisbane or online. Choose a preferred time when you book.

Can you help with an ATO letter, audit or debt?

Yes. We deal with the ATO on your behalf, respond to reviews and audits, and can arrange payment plans if you owe money.
